FAQ

Questions we hear

Why is it cheaper than local agencies?

We are a small remote studio with no office costs, and we build on a proven set of templates that we adapt to your business. You get a custom-looking site without paying for weeks of meetings.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain carries your business name and the files are yours. We manage the technical side while you're on Website Care, and if you ever leave, we transfer the domain and hand over every file.

What do I need to send you?

Your logo if you have one, 5 to 15 photos, your services and prices, opening hours and your WhatsApp number. We can write the text for you.

Can I take card payments?

Yes. Shops and bookings can send a secure Stripe payment link after you confirm an order. For bigger stores we can move you to Shopify later.

How do we work together?

Everything happens on WhatsApp and short video calls, in English. You approve each step before we move on.

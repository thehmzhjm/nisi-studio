Our story
"My grandmother made soap from the last pressing of the season. I use the same olive oil from our trees near Polis, with nothing you can't pronounce."
Katerina, maker

Elaia started at a kitchen table in 2019. Every bar is still mixed, poured and cut by hand, in batches of 40.
